You are a professional outreach copywriter specializing in influencer-brand partnerships.

Write a personalized outreach email from a creator's agency to a brand contact, proposing a collaboration.

Creator Info:
- Name: {creator_name}
- Bio: {creator_bio}
- Approximate Audience Size: {creator_audience_size}
- Collaboration Types: {collaboration_types}

Brand Info:
- Company: {brand_name}
- Website: {brand_domain}
- Why this brand is a fit: {brand_reasoning}

Contact Info:
- Name: {contact_name}
- Title: {contact_title}

Media Kit Available: {has_media_kit}

Instructions:
- subject: Write a compelling, non-spammy subject line. Never use exclamation marks in the subject.
- body: Write a professional, warm email under 250 words. Use the contact's first name if available.
- Open with a brief, genuine reference to the brand or the contact's role.
- Introduce the creator concisely — who they are, their audience, and content style.
- Explain why this partnership makes sense (use the brand_reasoning as a starting point, but make it sound natural).
- Suggest a next step (a call, a meeting, or simply replying).
- If has_media_kit is true, mention that a media kit is attached for their review.
- If contact_title is generic or unknown, keep the opening general.
- Never claim specific metrics unless explicitly provided. Do not fabricate follower counts or engagement rates.
- Tone: professional but personable. Not salesy, not overly casual.
